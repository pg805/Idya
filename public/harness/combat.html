Grid world, free bodies. The tiles are still the world — terrain, objects, everything you paint — but units move continuously over them and attacks are real hitboxes. The attack is a one-tile thrust. WASD moves, the mouse aims, left click thrusts, right click shields, Q drops a ward.

The thrust

A thrust is a long thin rectangle, so it reaches but barely sweeps. A swing would be short and wide — that difference is the thing a grid could not express at reach 1.

Ward — a 3×3 buff tile

Q lays it on the tile your centre point is in, plus the eight around it. Your body is a circle that can straddle four tiles, so standing on it has to mean something exact — the centre decides, and you can feel the edge.

View

The real tilesets, painted the way the game paints them — dirt underneath, grass autotiled off the corner lattice, canopy over the top so a body behind a trunk is behind the leaves. Trees block movement.

Bodies

Are you hitting what you aimed at?

No wedge any more — aim is a real angle, so there is no direction to round off. An early click is still held until the thrust is ready.

What changed

Positions are floats in tile units, not tile indices. Collision is circles, attacks are oriented rectangles, and enemies telegraph their thrust in dark red before it goes live — that window is the only reason you can dodge.

Still absent: the triangle, resource, armour poles, taunt, abilities as objects.

No tick. Movement and attacks run on the frame clock, cooldowns are milliseconds. The real server would still tick and interpolate, but nothing the player feels depends on that rate any more.
