# Map ideas

A capture place for regions, features and environmental storytelling. Unsorted,
uncommitted, added to whenever something occurs. Same spirit as
[`ideas.md`](ideas.md) and [`battle-ideas.md`](battle-ideas.md).

The system this feeds is `world.md` §17: a world-scale feature layer placed before
terrain, and **zones** (named regions spanning many chunks, each carrying its own
difficulty, spawn roster, spawn budget and terrain character). No hand-authored
chunks outside the town. So what gets designed here is **zones and features**, not
squares.

---

## The one rule: show evidence, never the answer

Environmental storytelling is the right tool here for three reasons. It gives a
place character, it needs no narrative system, and **it does not touch gameplay**.

The rule that makes it work for a platform rather than a story game: **leave it
unresolved.** A burned-out camp with a single set of tracks leading away, and
nothing anywhere explaining it. The player invents what happened, and their
invention is theirs.

This is the same discipline `lore/apolis.md` already applies to the glossary:
*state what a thing is and stop.* No asides, no knowing implications, no lines
that nudge toward an action. A place that tells you what happened there has
spent its content once. A place that shows you wreckage keeps paying out every
time somebody new walks in and has a theory.

Corollaries:

- **No notes, no journals, no readable plaques.** The moment there is text, it is
  narrative and it is imposed.
- **No resolution anywhere else either.** If the answer exists in a doc, someone
  will find it and the ambiguity dies for everybody.
- **Two readings should both fit.** If only one explanation is possible, that is
  just a story delivered slowly.
- **Players adding to it is the best outcome.** If land and building happen,
  evidence left by players is the version of this that scales.

---

## The other half: a region needs a reason to exist

Character does not come from art alone. A place is distinct when it **wants
something from you and gives something you cannot get elsewhere**. Flatness is
what happens when every location offers the same thing at the same cost.

So every region should be able to answer:

- **What does it give?** Ideally one faucet nobody else has (`world.md`: one
  faucet per resource).
- **What does it cost?** Travel, food, energy, gear wear, time.
- **What is the risk?** What lives there, and what it takes from you if it wins.
- **Why would a group come here rather than a person?** If there is no answer,
  it is solo content, and solo content is not where effort goes.

## Region template

Copy this per idea. Everything is optional except Gives / Costs / Hook.

```
### <name>

- **Read:**     what you see from a distance; the silhouette that pulls you
- **Gives:**    the thing only here
- **Costs:**    what being here takes
- **Threat:**   what lives here
- **Evidence:** what is lying around, unexplained
- **Hook:**     why a group comes, not a person
```

## Seeds

Prompts to be replaced, not proposals. They exist to show the format and to prime
the pump. Materials and enemies referenced are the ones already in
`database/`.

### The drowned works

- **Read:** standing water where water should not be, and roofs breaking the surface.
- **Gives:** water access, and whatever is still down there.
- **Costs:** slow going; everything takes longer.
- **Threat:** swallows (`world.md`: swallows spawn around water).
- **Evidence:** the water is not natural and nothing says why. Something was built
  here first and the water came second. Whether it was done on purpose is the
  question nobody can settle.
- **Hook:** what is worth taking is under, and going under means somebody stays up.

### A talamite cut

- **Read:** an open face in the rock, worked and then abandoned.
- **Gives:** talamite, the blacksmith tier-2 line.
- **Costs:** labour time, which pools and cannot be spent mid-fight.
- **Threat:** whatever moved in after.
- **Evidence:** the extraction was industrial in scale and stopped abruptly. Tools
  left where they were set down. Nothing looted. Nobody came back for any of it.
- **Hook:** the cut is worth more worked than raided, and working it is pooled time.

### Old growth

- **Read:** trees far larger than anything around them, visible from well outside.
- **Gives:** hardwood, the tier-3 carpentry line.
- **Costs:** the trees take real time to bring down and the noise carries.
- **Threat:** melbear (L4).
- **Evidence:** a ring of stumps at the edge, old, and none further in. Somebody
  started and decided not to continue.
- **Hook:** felling costs time you cannot spend fighting, so somebody has to not be
  chopping.

### Wherever the sidaev line comes from

- **Read:** unclear on purpose; endgame ground.
- **Gives:** nodol, the enchanter tier-3 line.
- **Costs:** high, and worth pricing so that going alone is a bad idea rather than a
  forbidden one.
- **Threat:** child of sidaev (L5), sulgovenath (L6).
- **Evidence:** open. Worth being the most withheld place on the map; the enemies
  named for it are already in the roster and nothing explains the name.
- **Hook:** the L5/L6 roster is balanced against upgraded and enchanted kit, which is
  several professions' worth of other people's work.

## Open questions

1. **How many zones?** Enough that each is distinct; few enough that each gets a
   personal pass. The number is a creative budget decision, not a systems one.
2. **Does a zone need a unique faucet to justify itself,** or can some exist purely
   as travel and character? One faucet per resource caps how many zones can be
   defined by what they give.
3. **Can players leave evidence?** Building, ruins, abandoned structures. This is the
   version of environmental storytelling that scales without authoring, and it
   depends on the land question in `world.md`.
4. **Does evidence need to persist identically for everyone,** or can a place change
   after enough people have passed through?
5. **How does a region read from outside it?** The pull comes from seeing something
   at distance and going to it. That is a rendering question the current chunk-sized
   viewport may not answer.
