# Where you are

Two continents are known.

The old continent holds the Chaevul Empire, its cities, and the war it is
currently fighting.

The Apolis continent is the other one. It is thick with magical creatures, and
with materials that carry sidaev in concentrations found almost nowhere else. You
are on it.

On its coast is a field with a few tents in it, an expedition camped around a
seam of ore. The scout who found the place named it **Sulku'it**, and the forest
beside it **Sulkupa**.

# The peoples

**The Chaevul** rule the empire. **The Ketulvu** are the people they rule.

The two live as one society now. The Chaevul lead, on the strength of what they
can build. They also keep Ketulvu traditions, mark Ketulvu occasions, and live in
a Ketulvu way, and each generation blurs the line further.

**Chae** means a person of Chaevul descent.

# Terms

**Sidaev.** The force that runs through living things, in different
concentrations. Some materials carry it better than others.

**Talamite.** A metal. Useful by itself, more useful alloyed, and it conducts
sidaev better than nearly anything. Rare on the old continent. Common on Apolis.

**Korel.** The currency of the Chaevul Empire.

**Padev.** The Ketulvu word for whoever runs a place, near enough to a mayor.
Both peoples use it.

# Who is here

Five groups joined the expedition.

**The Crown.** The Chae emperor. He is paying for the expedition, and the mine is
held in his name.

**The Keat Company.** A merchant house of ships and ledgers, running the
expedition on the crown's coin.

**The Kolem Thetholven.** Scientists and arcanists.

**The Scathlan States.** Allies of the Chaevul, fighting the same war.

**The Vidil Church.** The church of Vidali, god of life.

**Fendalok.** The padev of Sulku'it. Some Chae blood, some Ketulvu blood, a
commission from the crown, and a membership in the Keat Company.
