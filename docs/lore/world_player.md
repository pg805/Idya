# Places

**Apolis.** The continent the expedition is on. Thick with magical creatures, and
with materials that carry sidaev in concentrations found almost nowhere else.

**The old continent.** Where the Chaevul Empire sits, and where the war is being
fought. Talamite is rare there.

**Sulku'it.** The expedition site, on the Apolis coast. Currently a field, a few
tents, and a seam of ore.

**Sulkupa Forest.** The forest beside Sulku'it. Named, like Sulku'it, by the
scout who found the location.

# Peoples

**Chae.** A person of Chaevul descent.

**Chaevul.** The people who rule the empire.

**Ketulvu.** The people the Chaevul rule. The two live as one society: the
Chaevul lead, on the strength of what they can build, and also keep Ketulvu
traditions, mark Ketulvu occasions, and live in a Ketulvu way. Each generation
blurs the line further.

# Terms

**Korel.** The currency of the Chaevul Empire.

**Padev.** The Ketulvu word for whoever runs a place, near enough to a mayor.
Both peoples use it.

**Sidaev.** The force that runs through living things, in different
concentrations. Some materials carry it better than others.

**Talamite.** A metal. Useful by itself, more useful alloyed, and it conducts
sidaev better than nearly anything. Rare on the old continent, common on Apolis.

**The war.** The Chaevul Empire is currently at war with a country on the old
continent.

# Groups and people

**The Crown.** The Chae emperor. He is funding the expedition, and the mine is
held in his name.

**Fendalok.** The padev of Sulku'it. Some Chae blood, some Ketulvu blood, a
commission from the crown, and a membership in the Keat Company.

**The Keat Company.** A merchant house of ships and ledgers, running the
expedition on the crown's coin.

**The Kolem Thetholven.** Scientists and arcanists. Part of the expedition.

**The Scathlan States.** Allies of the Chaevul, fighting the same war. Part of
the expedition.

**The Vidil Church.** The church of Vidali. Part of the expedition.

**Vidali.** God of life.
